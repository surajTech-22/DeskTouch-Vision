# DeskTouch Vision v0.3.25 — Bidirectional Windows & Android keyboard and mouse sharing

DeskTouch Vision now reconnects **Windows PCs and Android phones** for wireless **keyboard and mouse sharing** over your private Wi-Fi network without Developer Options, cables, or Bluetooth dongles.

- **Two-way mouse and keyboard over Wi-Fi:** Use your PC keyboard & mouse seamlessly across connected Windows PCs and Android phones, or use your phone touchpad/keyboard to control Windows.
- **Screen-edge glide + Quick shortcut (`Ctrl + Alt + M`):** Connected Android phones appear as portrait screen tiles right inside `Displays`. Slide your cursor across the arranged edge or press **`Ctrl + Alt + M`** anytime to jump straight between PC and phone (clean modifier release, no stuck keys).
- **Fixed Android typing & mouse controls:** Fixed search bar, browser address bar, Enter/Tab, Arrow keys, Backspace/Delete, and `Ctrl+A/C/X/V` shortcuts on Android 13–15, plus Right Click (`Back`), Middle Click (`Home`), smooth drag timing, and touchpad click/scroll buttons in the phone app.
- **1-Tap Pause for Banking:** Pause phone control in one tap from the Quick Settings tile, ongoing notification, or inside the app before opening bank/UPI apps, while keeping Wi-Fi pairing ready.
- **Faster Wi-Fi auto-reconnect & cleaner logs:** Android listens for Wi-Fi network changes to reconnect automatically; Windows stops logging empty screen edges and keeps `connection.log` capped at 512 KB.
- **QR code installer:** Click **Get Android app** on Windows to scan and install the signed Android companion APK cleanly.
